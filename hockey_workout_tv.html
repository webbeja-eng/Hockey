Sets & Reps
4 × 5/side  ·  90 sec rest  ·  Stick landing 1 sec
Setup: BOSU dome-side up, 2–3 ft to the side.
Cues: Drive hard off one foot, land on dome on opposite foot. Hold 1 full second — load hip & knee, don't bounce off. Shorten distance before adding speed.

Sets & Reps
8 × 1 rep  ·  30 sec rest  ·  Max intent every set
Cues: Swing arms, load hips, explode up. Land softly — knees bent. Step down between reps, never jump down. Start at 18–20 in. Form first, height second.

Sets & Reps

Cues: Start in lunge — drop back knee, then explode up and scissor legs in the air. Arm drive is critical — pump hard. Land soft, absorb through the hip. Chest tall throughout.

Horizontal power
Sets & Reps
6 × 1 series  ·  60 sec rest  ·  Max distance
Cues: Two-foot takeoff, swing arms, drive knees up. Land and immediately reload — no pause between the three jumps. Stick the third landing and hold. Measure distance each session.

Couch Stretch
Hip flexor Quad release
Duration
90 sec/side  ·  Active glute squeeze — not passive
Setup: Rear foot up on bench/wall, front foot in lunge, rear knee on floor.
Cues: Squeeze glute of rear leg, drive hip forward and down. Active: squeeze 3 sec, release, sink further. Chest up, core on. Sends him to the hill loose and primed.

Outside — Hill work progression Always open with 2–3 bodyweight sprints regardless of week
Weeks 1–2
Bodyweight
Hill Sprint
Acceleration
Protocol
6 × 20–30 yds  ·  2 min full rest
Cues: Explosive start, drive knees high, pump arms like pistons, balls of the feet. Lean into the hill. Full rest every rep — if gasping by rep 3, rest longer. Speed, not cardio.
Watch on YouTube
Weeks 3–4
Weighted Vest
Hill Sprint
Vest load
Protocol
4–6 × 15–20 yds  ·  2–3 min full rest
Load: 5–8% bodyweight (~5–8 lbs). Never above 10% at this age.
Cues: Same mechanics — shorten to 15 yds with vest. Drop weight immediately if form breaks. 2 bodyweight sprints first to prime pattern.
Watch on YouTube
Weeks 5+
Belt Drag
Hill Sprint
Drag load Heaviest
Protocol
4 × 10–15 yds  ·  3 min full rest
Load: Start light — hill already adds resistance. Combined load should slow him ~30–40% max.
Cues: Max forward lean from ankles. Drive knee hard, push ground back. Arm drive — fists to chin. Short, explosive strides.
Watch on YouTube
Set 1 logic Lateral → vertical power, then 90/90 lets the nervous system reset between explosive blocks while opening the hips.
Set 2 logic Single-leg → horizontal power, then couch stretch releases the anterior chain so he arrives at the hill with full stride length.
Hill rule #1 Rest is the training. 2–3 min between every rep. Cut it short and you're doing conditioning — the opposite of speed development.
Hill rule #2 Always open with 2–3 bodyweight sprints first, even in weeks 5+. Pattern first, load second, every session.
